# Colloquium style

Read this file only when preparing or revising a colloquium. It holds the talk-type guidance and its visual defaults; general workflow and PowerPoint mechanics remain shared.

## Audience and argument

Audience: a whole Physics and Astronomy department, including theorists, experimentalists, faculty, postdocs, graduate students and sometimes advanced undergraduates. The accessibility reference is a first-year graduate student outside the speaker's subfield, while specialists should also learn something substantive.

Define subfield jargon when introduced. Explain why the problem matters before presenting specialized methods. Motivation and background may take a third to a half of the talk when the subject requires it; this is a planning guide, not a fixed quota. Keep the bulk of the argument accessible to the reference audience. The final third may go deeper for experts, then return to a broadly understandable conclusion.

At chapter and slide review, ask whether a colleague outside the field could explain the motivation, follow the evidence and remember the intended takeaway. Prefer fewer well-explained takeaways over many unsupported or hurried claims. State prerequisite concepts and introduce them before the result that needs them. Keep audience explanation in spoken notes where it would crowd the slide.

Audience requirements govern assumed knowledge even when the speaker normally uses specialist terminology.

## Visual defaults for Gordon

When the selected speaker profile is `gordon`, use PowerPoint's plain built-in default template and layouts with a **black master/layout background, white regular-weight text, and the standard Office theme palette**, unless a template or explicit style is supplied. Inherit formatting through native themes, masters and placeholders. Keep corresponding layouts consistent across related slides to reduce visual adjustment for the audience.

Gordon's [visual profile](../speakers/gordon/visual.md) supplies fonts, emphasis colors, geometry, composition patterns, citations and footer settings. His [content profile](../speakers/gordon/content.md) supplies voice and slide-writing preferences. Load each only for the relevant phase. Another speaker retains their own visual and content choices; this profile does not assign Gordon's identity or style to them.

Use the shared [PowerPoint mechanics](../../references/powerpoint.md) for native construction and editing. These colloquium settings do not become defaults for other talk types.
